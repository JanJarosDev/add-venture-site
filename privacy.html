Privacy policy

Add Venture · effective 14 October 2026

The short version: Add Venture doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account.

What stays on your device

The names, colors, scores, games, presets and settings you enter are saved in a database on your phone or tablet, so games can be resumed and history shown. Add Venture never sends them anywhere, and the developer can't see them.

Your device's own backup, such as iCloud Backup or Google's Android backup, may include this data under Apple's or Google's terms. Deleting the app deletes it from the device.

Purchases

Add Venture Plus is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; the developer never receives your payment details or who you are. The app only asks the store whether Plus has been bought, so it can unlock or restore it.

Ratings and sharing

The app may show the store's own rating prompt once. That's an Apple or Google feature, and the app doesn't learn whether or how you rated it.

When you share a score sheet, the picture and text go only where you choose to send them with your device's share sheet.

No tracking

Add Venture has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server of its own. It doesn't ask for your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Children

Add Venture is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because no personal data reaches the developer, there's nothing held about you to access, correct, export or delete. The data on your device is yours, and deleting the app removes it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
